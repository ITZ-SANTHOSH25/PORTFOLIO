PROJECT 01 FULL-STACK PLATFORM

Full-Stack Crowdfunding Platform for Children's Medical Treatment

Developed a full-stack crowdfunding platform to support children requiring medical treatment.

01

Hospital Portal

Registered hospitals can log in, submit pediatric medical cases, upload supporting documents, and provide estimated treatment costs.

02

Government Health Authority Portal

Authorized officers review applications, conduct on-site hospital visits, verify medical records and expenses, and approve or reject cases.

03

Public Crowdfunding

Donors can browse verified medical cases and contribute towards children's treatment.

Note: Hospital and Authority dashboards are restricted to authorized credentials stored in MongoDB. One account for each role is configured for demonstration.

PROJECT 02 EMERGENCY COORDINATION

Tamil Nadu Blood Bank Availability Platform (TNBA)

Developed a blood bank availability tracking platform to support faster medical emergency coordination across Tamil Nadu.

01

Problem Solved

Reduces delays caused by hospitals manually contacting multiple blood banks to find available blood units.

02

Key Functionality

Enables hospitals to search for specific blood groups and identify nearby facilities with available stock, helping save valuable time during emergencies.
